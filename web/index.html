Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom. Every pixel is computed on your CPU by C++ compiled to WebAssembly; WebGL is only used to show the finished frame.

Tile-based, parallel

Triangles are binned into 64×64 tiles and each tile is rasterized by one worker, so no locks are needed and the image is identical for any thread count. Try the thread slider and the heatmap.

Correct rasterization

Fixed-point edge functions with the top-left fill rule, homogeneous clipping and perspective-correct interpolation. Pixels are shaded in 2×2 quads, so mip derivatives come from neighboring lanes, as on a GPU.

SIMD everywhere

One 4-wide vector type maps to SSE2, ARM NEON and WebAssembly SIMD128: shading, texture filtering, shadow PCF and the sky are vectorized. Toggle it to compare with the scalar shader.

Physically based

Cook-Torrance GGX with metallic/roughness materials, normal mapping, soft PCF shadow maps, sky lighting and ACES tone mapping, all as C++ "shaders".
